SOXLayer: Simplifying SOX Compliance and Audit Management with AI-Powered Software

Managing compliance and internal audits can be challenging for businesses, especially when teams rely on spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected documents. internal controls testing software offers a modern solution designed to simplify SOX compliance, streamline control testing, and improve audit documentation through an integrated software platform.

SOXLayer brings essential compliance activities together in one workspace. Organizations can manage risk and control matrices, create testing plans, review supporting evidence, track deficiencies, and prepare audit-ready reports. This connected approach helps reduce repetitive administrative work and makes it easier for teams to maintain organized compliance records.

One of the platform's key features is AI-assisted control testing. Instead of manually reviewing every document, users can leverage AI to evaluate supporting evidence and suggest testing results. Each proposed result includes the relevant quotation and page reference, allowing reviewers to understand how the assessment was reached. Importantly, human reviewers retain control and can accept, edit, or override AI recommendations with documented reasons.

Evidence management and audit trails are also important parts of the SOXLayer experience. The platform uses SHA-256 file hashing and a hash-chained activity log to support document integrity and traceability. Teams can track changes, identify responsible users, and review previous actions. Role-based permissions also help organizations manage access for preparers, reviewers, approvers, and external auditors.

Another useful capability is deficiency management. When testing identifies an exception, teams can connect it to a deficiency, assign remediation responsibilities, establish deadlines, and track retesting progress. Dashboards provide visibility into overdue tests, open deficiencies, and overall testing progress.

For organizations seeking a more structured approach to financial control testing, SOXLayer offers a practical combination of automation, documentation, and human oversight. As an internal controls testing software solution, it helps organizations bring compliance activities into one platform, reduce spreadsheet dependency, and build a more consistent, transparent, and traceable audit process.
